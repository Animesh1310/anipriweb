How this website handles project inquiries.

Information you provide

The inquiry form collects your name, email, project description, and any optional company, service, website, budget, or timeline details you provide. Please do not submit passwords, identity documents, payment information, or sensitive client information.

How information is used

Inquiry details are used to review your request, communicate with you, and plan potential services. When online inquiries are enabled, the form is processed by Formspree and delivered through its service to the configured business recipient. Formspree may store submissions under its own privacy policy. The website itself is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Analytics

When configured, Google Analytics loads only if you choose to allow optional analytics. It measures page visits and successful inquiry submissions; form contents are not included in these events. Use the contact form to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you submitted. Include your inquiry reference if you have one, and avoid posting sensitive information.
